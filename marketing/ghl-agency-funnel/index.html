Questions.

Can I try it with one client first?

Yes. Create your agency, connect one Google account, add one business. No minimum, nothing to install. There is no free trial, so you pay £149 and £5 for that one business, and can cancel whenever you like.

What does my client see?

Your name, logo, colour and domain. Two exceptions: Google's own permission screen shows the name of the software asking, and the printable guide offered after the free check carries ours. Everything else is yours.

How does it connect to GHL?

In Settings, paste a Private Integration token for your sub-account. It is checked when you save it. Each agency's token only ever sends that agency's mail.
