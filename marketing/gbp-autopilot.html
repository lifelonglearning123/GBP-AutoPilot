GBP AutoPilot

Keeps a business's Google Business Profile complete and answered, and shows where it actually ranks in local search — for the agencies and businesses we run local SEO for.

What GBP AutoPilot is

GBP AutoPilot is software built by Macaws.ai for managing Google Business Profiles. A profile that is complete, answered and posted to regularly is the difference between a business appearing in the local map results and being invisible there — but keeping one that way is a weekly chore that nobody gets round to. GBP AutoPilot does the chore and reports what changed.

It works on profiles whose owner has connected their own Google account and given us access, and on nothing else. There is no scraping and no shortcut: a profile is either connected by the person who manages it, or it is not in the tool.

What it does

Scores the profile out of 100

Every field Google offers — categories, opening hours, services, description, photos, phone, website — checked and scored, with the work that would gain the most points listed first.

Fills in what is missing

Drafts the description, the service list and the categories, shows them for approval, then writes the approved version back to Google. Nothing is sent without a person pressing the button.

Answers reviews

Drafts a reply to every new review in the business's own voice. Replies can post automatically or wait for approval; poor ratings always wait, and raise an alert.

Posts every week

Writes and schedules the weekly Google post that keeps a profile active, so the listing does not go quiet between jobs.

Tracks local ranking

Checks where the business appears for the searches its customers actually use, across a grid of points around the premises, and shows what moved since last time.

Reports in plain English

A client-ready report of what was done, what it changed and what is still worth doing — written for a business owner, not an SEO specialist.

How it uses your Google account

GBP AutoPilot signs in with Google and asks for one permission: business.manage, which Google describes as "See, edit, create and delete your Google business listings". That is the only permission it requests, and it is used strictly for the profiles you choose to manage in the tool.

What we readWhy
Your profile details Name, address, phone, website, categories, opening hours, services and description — to score the profile and show what is missing.
Your reviews To draft replies, flag poor ratings quickly, and report on rating and volume over time.
Your photos and performance figures To check the profile has recent photos, and to report how many people called, clicked or asked for directions.
Your email address Only to show which Google account is connected, so you can tell two accounts apart.
What we writeWhen
Profile fields Phone, website, hours, categories, description and services — only the ones you approve, only when you press save.
Review replies Either when you approve the draft, or automatically if you have switched that on for this business.
Posts When a scheduled post you have approved is due, or when you publish one by hand.

We never sell your data, and we never use it to train anyone's models. Google data is used to run the service for you and for nothing else. Disconnect the account in the tool and access ends immediately — the token is revoked with Google at that moment. Our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

Who it is for

Local businesses whose customers find them on Google Maps — trades, clinics, salons, contractors — and the marketing agencies that look after them. Agencies use it across every client they manage, under their own name.
